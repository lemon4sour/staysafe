title: Masked Links
author: Rolimon's Community
pubDate: 2025-08-12
---
This type of scam involves a user sending a link to another user. They use Discord's link masking feature to hide a scam link and make it look like a real genuine Roblox or Rolimons link.

You can check if the link is fake or not by hovering your mouse over it. If it is fake, it will show two different URLs.

Even if the link looks safe or you can't check it because of the mobile app, it's always safer to avoid clicking it. Regardless, if you randomly get sent a link , it's more than likely a scam.
