title: Double Trade Scam
author: Rolimon's Community
pubDate: 2025-08-01
---

In this scam, a person will send you two trades: one with Robux and one without. The scammer will spend their Robux right after sending it, so the trade with Robux is rejected and the one without goes through. Please double check before doing these types of trades.
