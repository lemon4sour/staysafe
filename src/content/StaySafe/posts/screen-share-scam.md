This type of scam involves a user faking evidence of you doing something bad to get you banned from a server. Once you are banned, they impersonate a staff member of that server and ask you to confirm account ownership by screensharing your Reset Password Link (or other forms of personal information). They then gain access to your account and take your items. Always check for impersonators by confirming their tags and developer IDs with the real ones, and do not screenshare vulnerable information.
