These scams are where a user DMs the victim with a forged and fake link leading to a malicious website. This could be any sort of website, including fake Nitro links, fake Steam links, and fake Roblox profile/game/group links.

These sites usually attempt to steal your account by prompting you for your account information, trying to make you run some malicious script code, or using some other scheme.

The most common scams for this are fake Roblox links, fake Steam gift card links, and fake free Nitro links. They may also use the [Masked Links](</posts/masked-links>) Method to trick you into clicking these links.
