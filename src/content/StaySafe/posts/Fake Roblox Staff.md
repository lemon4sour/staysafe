_This post is kept for archival reasons: the messaging feature on Roblox has been discontinued._

A fake Roblox admin account messages you on Roblox, saying you were reported for termination due to gains in value. They will ask you to add their Discord and request you to add their email account on the forwarding link. This will send them every email you receive, including two- step verification codes and password reset links when you try to log in.
